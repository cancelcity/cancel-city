# First-session progression and interaction polish

The first session now continues through five milestones: collect a first pack, finish five guided combat lessons, win a scored battle, collect a pack after that victory, then review and save Squad 1. Practice is optional and remains separate from account rewards. A loss offers another attempt without restarting completed lessons.

The existing authoritative command flow records the first victory's pack count, reward-pack acknowledgment and deliberate squad save in a defaulted `career.starter` field. Opening alone does not finish the reward milestone. Saving a preset before collecting a post-victory pack does not skip it. These steps grant no extra currency, XP or cards. Older saves decode with defaults; established winners are not forced back through the guide.

The lobby loads a saved lineup or the last battle crew, with owned characters and starter loaners as a fallback. Its launch controls precede the optional tactics catalogue. The default twelve-card deck is ready immediately. Choosing an unselected fighter from a full roster opens a replacement comparison with HP, ATK, DEF, star-cost changes and the new Special. Replacements change only the selected lineup; Save Squad 1 persists it. A reviewed crew can be kept: a random pack is not guaranteed to contain a stronger fighter, and foil copies do not provide combat bonuses.

Victory makes the reward pack the primary action. Practice results and completed lessons lead to campaign preparation. Sharing remains available in a disclosure. Home uses short visible labels with full accessible action descriptions so saved-match and reward instructions fit the illustrated layout.

Gemini's visual effects and district tactics are retained. Idle pack effects stop when complete, idle scene rendering uses a lower cadence, card tilt respects both motion preferences and hover input, and city pause preserves camera position. Trap reactions use a compact nonblocking notification; repeated ready-state pulses are removed.

Verification focuses on save compatibility, first-win to collected-pack to saved-squad progression, tactical rules and deck construction, followed by production compilation and representative desktop/mobile browser checks. This is an implementation check, not evidence of a completed independent human beta.

Browser validation completed the existing first pack, five lessons, a full campaign victory, post-win pack acknowledgment, a replacement comparison and Squad 1 save. Home was checked at desktop and 390 px mobile viewport with no horizontal overflow. City zoom/pan coordinates stayed identical when pause was toggled.
