# Gokart layered rebuild

The user identified two related defects: visible frame-by-frame stepping and scenery changing between generated drawings. This rebuild separates the shot into fixed scenery plates, intact alpha characters/karts, and separately animated effects. It preserves the corrected scratchy neon card art.

Two fixed background images are reused across the sequence. Character alpha sheets preserve each shot's whole-body drawings; their transparent bounds are measured to keep the actor grounded while continuous position/scale/rotation trajectories run at 60fps. Aura shapes, road streaks, exhaust smoke, barrier tires, dust and the final loose wheel are separate deterministic animated objects. The film is retimed from 9.6s to 5.8s and the original self-produced soundtrack is retimed per shot. No detached body-part rig is introduced.

The tested full-frame motion interpolation introduced doubled outlines and was rejected. Dense optical-flow tests also distorted the background and contours. Neither technique is in the published renderer. The ignored work/ directory contains those experiments only. A video-generation integration was suggested during exploration but was not connected or used.

Raster extraction and scenery production use built-in imagegen with original sheets as edit targets. Original outputs, prompts and alpha/crop metadata are preserved per folder. The renderer uses Canvas for compositing and code-native effects; it never regenerates a background between frames. Files are versioned under public/assets/gokart/layered-v3/. Scene metadata is src/data/gokart-film.json; rebuild with scripts/render-gokart-layered.mjs.

Some generated alpha sheets failed their nominal cell boundaries. The source alpha objects are therefore separated mechanically by connected components; merged/overlapping subjects are excluded rather than repainted. `scripts/prepare-gokart-layers.mjs` preserves source pixels, crops complete disconnected actors and records every rectangle in component-extraction.json. Current runtime uses 40 complete drawings: ignition8, reveal2, drift9, rush6, impact7 and ending8. The quiet reveal deliberately reuses two clean poses while its movement/effects remain continuous. The detached ending wheel is excluded from the main actor crop and animated separately. Exact generation prompts are in the intro/action/ending/impact/scenery subfolders. Source originals, including rejected extraction attempts, are retained for traceability.

Validation includes exact background-only hashes with locked camera at different times, alpha coverage, all referenced assets, media decoding, codecs/duration/frame count, continuous actor trajectories and TypeScript/production builds. Browser, phone and audible playback remain unverified because the managed browser-control capability is unavailable. Discrete expression/pose drawings remain visible in places; this is not a claim of fully hand-drawn studio animation.
