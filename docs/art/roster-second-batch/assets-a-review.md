# Second roster batch: artwork A

Built-in image_gen produced four dedicated opaque expressive square portraits and four matching transparent six-pose atlases for CC003 L Ratio, CC004 UsoppGPT, CC005 Sauron Surveillance and CC008 Sully Sullivan HR. The existing CC001 and CC014 corrected portraits were inspected and used as style references. Exact prompts and output source paths are recorded in each character's provenance.json and the jobs manifests.

Portraits: public/assets/roster-b2/CC003.webp, CC004.webp, CC005.webp, CC008.webp, each with a -small.webp sibling. Actors: public/assets/actors/CCxxx-b2-0.webp through -5.webp. Each character folder contains actor.json, original-portrait.png, original-sheet.png and provenance.json.

All 24 runtime crops were visually inspected. An extra arm in the original L recoil drawing was rejected and repaired by imagegen; the corrected sheet has exactly two attached arms and two hands in that pose. Cell boundaries were adjusted mechanically where held props or wide stances extend beyond nominal thirds. The Usopp anticipation crop initially contained a tiny piece of the next cell's phone glow; its row boundary was adjusted and re-inspected. No body parts were cut apart or rigged. Feet and tower base anchors were marked manually, and one common baseHeight per actor preserves smaller crouches.

Sauron's six expressions remain attached to an intact whole tower. Wide release flames reach the outer sheet margin; no tower body or base is clipped. Sully's wide roar uses a wider crop to preserve both entire hands and claws. Sully portraits/sheet each required an identical retry after a benign output moderation error. All requested assets ultimately generated successfully.

Asset review covers anatomy, whole bodies, props, crop boundaries and alpha presence. Full-speed arena staging and mobile playback are integration checks for the Site owner.
